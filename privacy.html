FDH Tracking Sync: Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026.

FDH Tracking Sync ("the app") is a private automation built and operated by Dialytics Consulting for a single business customer. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

  • Gmail: with the account owner's permission, the app reads emails in one dedicated mailbox that receives supplier invoices, including their PDF attachments, and applies labels to mark emails as processed or needing review. It uses the gmail.modify scope only for these purposes. It does not send email on the account's behalf, delete email, or read other mailboxes.
  • Online store: the app looks up the matching order and adds shipment tracking numbers to it.

How the data is used

The app extracts order references and shipment tracking numbers from supplier invoices and adds the tracking numbers to the matching orders. Data is used only to provide this function to the business that authorized it.

Sharing

Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties, and it is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. The app sends tracking numbers only to the business's own online store.

Storage and retention

The app processes emails as it runs and does not keep copies of email content. It keeps a run log of the actions it takes (for example, which order received which tracking number) so the business can review them. Access credentials are stored in encrypted secret storage and are never published.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, the app can no longer read the mailbox.
