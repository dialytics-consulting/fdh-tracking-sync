FDH Tracking Sync

FDH Tracking Sync is a private automation built and operated by Dialytics Consulting for a single business customer. It is not a public product and has no sign-up.

It reads supplier invoice emails in one dedicated mailbox, extracts shipment tracking numbers from the attached invoices, and adds those tracking numbers to the matching orders in the business's online store.
